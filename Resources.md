## AI : 10 GitHub Repos to learn about AI
1. [Hands-On Large Language Models](https://github.com/HandsOnLLM/Hands-On-Large-Language-Models): Full set of notebooks from the book, covers everything from LLM basics to advanced fine-tuning
2. [AI Agents for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/ai-agents-for-beginners): A free 11-part beginner series that guides you through creating your first working agent
3. [GenAI Agents](https://github.com/NirDiamant/GenAI_Agents): Tutorials and code for building generative AI agents, from simple workflows to complex architectures
4. [Made with ML](https://github.com/GokuMohandas/Made-With-ML): Practical lessons on how to design, build, deploy, and iterate on production-ready ML applications
5. [Prompt Engineering Guide](https://github.com/dair-ai/Prompt-Engineering-Guide): A goldmine of resources, papers, and tutorials on mastering effective prompt design
6. [Hands-On AI Engineering](https://github.com/Sumanth077/Hands-On-AI-Engineering): Showcases of LLM-powered apps and agent solutions you can replicate and adapt
7. [Awesome Generative AI Guide](https://github.com/aishwaryanr/awesome-generative-ai-guide): Curated hub of learning materials, tools, and research on generative AI
8. [Designing Machine Learning Systems](https://github.com/chiphuyen/dmls-book): Summaries, examples, and extra material from the book on building scalable ML systems
9. [Machine Learning for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/ML-For-Beginners): Free and beginner-friendly, introduces essential ML concepts with hands-on practice
10. [LLM Course](https://github.com/mlabonne/llm-course): Roadmaps and notebooks that teach you how to design, build, and deploy applications with LLMs
