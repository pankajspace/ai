AI Reliability Lab

Measure the levers that turn a flaky model into a dependable component

🎲 Variance & Determinism

Extracts sentiment and entities from a review 5 times under each of three strategies (unconstrained, prompt asks for JSON, API-enforced schema) at temperature 0.7, then grades every output with a strict json.loads() parser.

🧠 Chain-of-Thought vs Direct

Runs one multi-step reasoning problem 5 times with a direct prompt and 5 times with "think step by step", scoring only the final line. Presets: grid, family, schedule, inventory, boxes.

🧭 Tool Schema Calibration

Offers three weather tools (current, forecast, history) under a 2x2 of descriptive vs opaque names and loose vs tight descriptions. Your question is routed under all four, then 12 labelled queries fill in the accuracy matrix.

🚨 Tool Error Injection

An agent answers with a get_weather tool whose second call always returns HTTP 503. Compare a plain prompt with one that demands error reporting: did the model admit the failure, give the code, or invent weather? Ask about two or more cities.
