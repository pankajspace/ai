See inside your AI agent, then keep it safe in production

1. **[01. LLM Observability Explained](observability-and-guardrails-1-observability-explained.html)** — Why monitoring isn't enough, the MELT framework (metrics, events, logs, traces), tracing an agent, a debugging story about an agent that lies, and choosing tools like LangSmith.
