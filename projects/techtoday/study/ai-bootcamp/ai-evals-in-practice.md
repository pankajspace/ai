# AI Evals in Practice

A complete evals playbook, from your first eval to governance, plus a system design case study

1. **[01. Why AI Evals](ai-evals-in-practice-1-why-ai-evals.html)** — What an eval is, why leaderboards and bought tools aren't enough, and how a real team plans, scopes and staffs an evals project, with your first eval in code.
2. **[02. Evaluation Methods & Qualitative Evals](ai-evals-in-practice-2-evaluation-methods-and-qualitative-evals.html)** — The whole evals toolbox beyond LLM-as-a-judge, then the step every project starts with: reading real outputs and building an error taxonomy.
3. **[03. Metrics, AI Judges & Test Sets](ai-evals-in-practice-3-quantitative-evals-metrics-ai-judges-and-test-sets.html)** — Turn the error taxonomy into numbers: automatic metrics, AI judges you can trust, and test sets sized to support a launch decision, with Python code calling OpenAI.
4. **[04. After Launch: Experiments & Monitoring](ai-evals-in-practice-4-after-launch-experiments-monitoring-and-business-impact.html)** — Pilot with real users, monitor the live system, and measure business impact to answer the question every leader asks about an AI feature.
5. **[05. Tools, Governance & Advanced Topics](ai-evals-in-practice-5-tools-governance-and-advanced-topics.html)** — Which eval tools to use and when, turning governance and compliance rules into tests, and evaluating multi-turn conversations, images, audio and fuzzy qualities.
6. **[06. Case Study: Adaptive Learning App](ai-evals-in-practice-case-study-adaptive-learning-app.html)** — LLM System Design chapter 4: design a Duolingo-style app that writes and personalises lessons for 50 million learners, from requirements and APIs to testing and monitoring.
